The Penny Dreadful Scoreboard

This world plays Penny Dreadful: a dreadful, horrific game played by flipping pennies! The scoreboard opens on every screen when you join.

  • Players can skip the Director's section: click here to learn how to use the scoreboard.
  • Anyone can read Penny Dreadful's rules. The book icon on the scoreboard opens them too.

For the Director

In Penny Dreadful the game master is called the Director, and this world renames Foundry's Game Master role to match. Here is what the Director needs to know:

  • Dice So Nice is recommended. It isn't required, but with that module active the pennies tumble across every screen on each flip, and the scoreboard gains the hold and clear buttons described below. Try it, you'll like it!
  • PCs for the players: every player gets a Penny Dreadful character named after them the first time they connect, in the Actors tab's Penny Dreadful folder and marked (PC-PD).
  • Closed your scoreboard with the X? Click the coins in the Token controls on the left, or press Alt+B, to bring it back.
  • Ask a player for a flip: click a DL number (1 to 5) on their row. The ask goes to chat as a card with a Flip button, and the row is spotlit. Click the same number again to withdraw it. A DL higher than the pennies the row holds can't be asked, because it couldn't be met.
  • Add or remove pennies by hand: − and + on a row. Minus on a dead row brings the character back to life first.
  • Spotlight: the star on a row, or the skip-forward button in the toolbar to spotlight the next row.
  • Add a PC or NPC: the person-plus (PC) and stranger (NPC) buttons in the toolbar. NPCs land in the Penny Dreadful folder too, marked (NPC-PD). The slashed-person button on a row takes the character off the scoreboard; nothing is deleted, and you can add it back.
  • Choose who plays a PC, or rename a character: click its name on the scoreboard to open it, then pick the Player or edit the name (25 characters at most).
  • Hold the pennies on screen: with Dice So Nice active, the push-pin toggle keeps each flip's pennies on every screen for dramatic effect until you sweep them away with the broom.
  • Clear the cast for a new one-shot: the clapperboard deletes every PC, clears the chat log, benches the NPCs, and makes a fresh PC for each connected player. It asks first.
  • What are the chances? The % icon opens a table of the odds of success for every DL against the pennies in a player's hand.
  • Settings: the NPC Penny Limit (5 to 10) and automatic PCs are in Configure Settings → Penny Dreadful.

For Players

  • You get a PC named after you when you join. Click your own name on the scoreboard to change your character's name.
  • When the Director asks you to flip, a card appears in the chat with a Flip button, and the same button appears on your row. Click it once.
  • Heads count toward the Difficulty Level (DL). Meet or beat it and you succeed. Fail and you gain a penny. Failing while holding ten pennies is the end of your rope. The rules have the rest, and the odds table shows your chances.
  • You can close your scoreboard with its X; Alt+B or the coins in the Token controls bring it back.
