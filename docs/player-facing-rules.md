This is a Horror Movie. The game master is called "The Director."

🪙 Here is my adaptation of the complete player facing ruleset for the game's system, "Penny Dreadful"

- You have a character, but you don't have a character sheet.
-  Your character has a name, a past that they shared with the other players, and hopes for the future.
- As the story unfolds, you will investigate and interrogate the world and interact with it in realistic ways. 
-  When you take an action, or linger in in-action, the Director may ask you to make check or save by flipping pennies!

🪙 There is only one player's mechanic in this system!
- Each player starts with one penny. 
-  When the director (your Game Master) ask you make a save or check, they will give you a Difficulty Score (DS) than you need to meet or beat to succeed. Then they will ask you to flip your pennies (by clicking a button in Foundry VTT in our case).
- Heads count 1 and Tails count 0 toward your success. 
- The difficulty score is variable, but the DS maximum is 5. 
- If the number of heads meets or beats the DS, you succeed.
- If you fail the check or save, you will be given an additional penny that you must flip on your next check or save. 
- You may accrue up to 10 pennies.

🪙 With 9 or less pennies, failing a check can just mean that doom comes closer, not necessarily that the action didn’t work. 
- You can break down that door, but the sound echoes…
- You can jump that gap, but they leave a small scrap of fabric on the cliff-side…

🪙 With 10 pennies, failing a check means that it's the end for your character. They die, usually in a spectacularly horrific way. 

🪙 Success on a check or save moves the players closer to their stated or implied goals.
