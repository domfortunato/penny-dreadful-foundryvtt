## The Penny Dreadful Dashboard

Penny Dreadful runs here as a mini game inside your campaign: a dreadful,
horrific interlude played by flipping pennies!

- Players can skip the Director's section: [click here](#for-players) to
  learn how to use the dashboard.
- Anyone can read [Penny Dreadful's rules](pd:rules). The book icon on the
  board opens them too.

## For the Director

In Penny Dreadful the game master is called the Director. Here is what the
Director needs to know:

- **Dice So Nice is recommended.** It isn't required, but with that module
  active the pennies tumble across every screen on each flip, and the board
  gains the hold and clear buttons described below. Try it, you'll like it!
- **Start the game:** click the coins in the Token controls on the left, and
  the board opens for you and every player. The coins do nothing without an
  active scene; then press Alt+B to open your board and choose **Start the
  Penny Dreadful Mini Game** from its header menu (⋮), or use Configure
  Settings → Penny Dreadful Mini Game Running.
- **PCs for the players:** when the game starts, every connected player who
  has no Penny Dreadful character yet gets one, named after them, in the
  Actors tab's Penny Dreadful folder and marked (PC-PD). Anyone who connects
  while the game runs gets one too.
- **Closed your board with the X?** Click the coins or press Alt+B to bring
  it back. The coins never end the game.
- **End the game:** the board's header menu (⋮) → **End the Penny Dreadful
  Mini Game**. It asks first. Nothing is deleted: start again and the board
  is as you left it.
- **Ask a player for a flip:** click a DS number (1 to 5) on their row. The
  ask goes to chat as a card with a Flip button, and the row is spotlit.
  Click the same number again to withdraw it. A DS higher than the pennies
  the row holds can't be asked, because it couldn't be met.
- **Add or remove pennies by hand:** − and + on a row. Minus on a dead row
  brings the character back to life first.
- **Spotlight:** the star on a row, or the skip-forward button in the
  toolbar to spotlight the next row.
- **Add a PC or NPC:** the person-plus (PC) and stranger (NPC) buttons in the
  toolbar. NPCs land in the Penny Dreadful folder too, marked (NPC-PD). The
  slashed-person button on a row takes the character off the board; nothing
  is deleted, and you can add it back.
- **Choose who plays a PC, or rename a character:** click its name on the
  board to open it, then pick the Player or edit the name (25 characters at
  most).
- **Hold the pennies on screen:** with Dice So Nice active, the push-pin
  toggle keeps each flip's pennies on every screen for dramatic effect until
  you sweep them away with the broom.
- **Clear the cast for a new one-shot:** the clapperboard deletes every
  Penny Dreadful PC and the mini game's own chat messages, benches the NPCs,
  and makes a fresh PC for each connected player. It asks first.
- **What are the chances?** The % icon opens [a table of the odds](pd:odds)
  of success for every DS against the pennies in a player's hand.

## For Players

- When the Director starts the game, the board opens and you get a PC named
  after you. Click your own name on the board to change your character's
  name.
- When the Director asks you to flip, a card appears in the chat with a
  **Flip** button, and the same button appears on your row. Click it once.
- Heads count toward the Difficulty Score (DS). Meet or beat it and you
  succeed. Fail and you gain a penny. Failing while holding ten pennies is
  the end of your character. [The rules](pd:rules) have the rest, and
  [the odds table](pd:odds) shows your chances.
- You can close your board with its X; Alt+B or the coins in the Token
  controls bring it back while the game is running.
