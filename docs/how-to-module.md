## The Mini Game

Penny Dreadful runs here as a mini game inside your campaign: a horror
interlude on its own board. Nothing happens in the campaign until the
Director starts it, and ending it closes the board on every screen.
The host campaign's own actors and chat are left alone.

## For the Director

- **Start the game:** click the coins in the Token controls on the left.
  The board opens for every player, and each connected player without a PC
  gets one. With no scene active the coins are not there: press Alt+B to
  open your board and choose **Start the Penny Dreadful Mini Game** from
  its header menu (⋮), or use Configure Settings → Penny Dreadful Mini
  Game Running.
- **Closed your board?** Click the coins or press Alt+B to bring it back.
  The coins never end the game.
- **End the game:** the board's header menu (⋮) → **End the Penny Dreadful
  Mini Game**. It asks first. Nothing is deleted: start again and the board
  is as you left it.
- **Ask for a flip:** click a DS number (1 to 5) on a row. The card goes to
  chat and the row is spotlit. Click the same number again to withdraw the
  ask. A DS above the pennies a row holds cannot be asked.
- **Pennies:** − and + on a row change its hand. Minus on a dead row brings
  it back to life first.
- **Spotlight:** the star on a row, or the next-row button in the toolbar.
- **Add a PC or NPC:** the person-plus and stranger buttons in the toolbar.
  The slashed-person button on a row takes it off the board; nothing is
  deleted, and you can add it back.
- **Who plays a PC:** open the PC (click its name) and choose the Player.
- **A new one-shot:** the clapperboard. It deletes every Penny Dreadful PC
  and the mini game's own chat messages, benches the NPCs, and makes a
  fresh PC for each connected player.
- In the Actors tab, the mini game's actors are marked (PC-PD) and (NPC-PD).

## For Players

- When the Director starts the game, the board opens and you get a PC
  named after you. Click your own name on the board to change your
  character's name.
- When the Director asks you to flip, a card appears in the chat with a
  **Flip** button, and the same button appears on your row. Click it once.
- Heads count toward the Difficulty Score (DS). Meet or beat it and you
  succeed. Fail and you gain a penny. Failing while holding ten pennies is
  the end of your character.
- You can close your board with its X; Alt+B brings it back while the game
  is running.

## The Toolbar

- Book: the rules.
- Percent: the odds of every flip.
- Question mark: this guide.
